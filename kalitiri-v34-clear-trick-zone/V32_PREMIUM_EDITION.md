# v32 Premium Edition

This release is a full visual/UX premium pass over v31 without changing the established game rules.

## Premium treatment
- Luxury emerald, obsidian and gold design language.
- Premium splash, brand badge and table watermark.
- High-contrast competitive HUD and glass side panels.
- More realistic cards, depth, shadows and legal-play feedback.
- Refined seats, timers, voice controls, result celebration and modals.
- Stronger desktop and mobile responsive behavior.
- 26-card hands remain auto-fit and gameplay content is kept clear of overlays.

## Retained gameplay
All v31 server-authoritative bidding, partner-card privacy/reveal, scoring, series, voice, reconnect, fair-play, ledger and room settings remain intact.
