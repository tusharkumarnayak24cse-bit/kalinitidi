# Kaali Ni Tidi v19 — 3 Player Support

The game now supports **3–8 players**.

## 3-player custom variant
- 1 reduced deck
- Remove all four 2s (zero-point cards)
- 48 cards total
- 16 cards per player / 16 tricks
- 250 total scoring points remain in play
- Bidding uses the normal one-deck range: 150–250 in +5 steps
- Bid winner chooses Hukum and calls 1 hidden partner card
- After the called card is revealed, the teams are bidder + partner vs one defender

This is a custom 3-player adaptation; it is not presented as part of the supplied 8-player KaliTiri rules page.
