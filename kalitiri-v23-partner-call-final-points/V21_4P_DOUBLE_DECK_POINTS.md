# v21 — 4-Player Double-Deck + Detailed Points

## 4-player deck choice
- 1 deck: 52 cards, 13 each, 250 total points, bidding 150–250.
- 2 decks: 104 cards, 26 each, 500 total points, bidding 300–500 in +5 increments.
- In either 4-player mode the bidder calls 1 hidden partner, producing a 2 vs 2 game after the partner is revealed.
- Quick Match and Ranked keep 4-player 1-deck and 2-deck queues separate.

## Point table
The live sidebar now shows the contract, bidder-team points, defense points, remaining points, and the complete point-card breakdown. The rules modal also shows the full one-deck and two-deck totals.

Point values: 3♠ = 30; A/K/Q/J/10 = 10 each; 5 = 5; all other cards = 0. One deck totals 250; two decks total 500.
